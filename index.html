Agenda Bot

Agenda Bot is a small personal, non-commercial tool. It lets its developer create Google Calendar events by sending a message to a private Telegram bot. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does

  • The developer writes a short message describing an appointment, for example "dentist tomorrow at 10".
  • After the developer confirms, the app creates one event in the developer's own Google Calendar and adds one attendee chosen by the developer.
  • Google Calendar then sends the invitation email to that attendee.

Google user data

The app asks for the Google Calendar permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events only to create events. It does not read, copy, analyse or share the events that already exist in the calendar. See the Privacy Policy for details on how Google user data is used and stored.
